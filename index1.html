Mole Concept Calculations
• Moles (n) = Given Mass (m) / Molar Mass (M)
• Particles = n × 6.022 × 10²³ (Avogadro Constant)
• Gas Volume at STP = n × 22.4 Litres (or 22,400 cm³)
• Vapor Density: Molar Mass = 2 × V.D.
Kinematics & Motion Equations
• v = u + at
• s = ut + ½ at²
• v² - u² = 2as
• Force F = m × a; Work W = F × s × cos(θ)
Algebra & Geometry
• Roots x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / (2a)
• Discriminant D = b² - 4ac (D > 0: real; D < 0: imaginary)
• (a² - b²) = (a - b)(a + b)
• Distance = √[(x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²]
English Grammar Core Rules
• Passive Voice: Obj + suitable 'to be' + Past Participle (V3)
• Indirect Speech: Present simple shifts to Past simple
• Conditionals: If + Past Perfect, would + have + V3
• Agreement: 'Either of the boys IS' (Singular verb)
